Pokémon Black / White – sources for the Unova map

WHITE
The White tiles were rendered locally from a user-provided German Pokémon
White Nintendo DS ROM (game code IRAD). The ROM is not copied into this
project. Karte/tools/render-unova.js reads only the required Nitro archives:

  a/0/0/8  map containers and BMD0 models
  a/0/0/9  world matrices (matrix 0)
  a/0/1/4  shared texture banks

The renderer rebuilt 229 occupied 32 x 32 map blocks from the original models
and textures. Karte/tools/make-tiles.py converted that render into the local
zoom sheets under white/.

BLACK FALLBACK
A Black ROM was not supplied. Black therefore keeps an aligned fallback made
from the Mewmaps composite until the matching game files are available:

  https://www.mewmaps.org/black-white
  https://drive.google.com/file/d/1n0lgrrQYa6t1LBhHGAJPbEGwVy7HjMUu/view
